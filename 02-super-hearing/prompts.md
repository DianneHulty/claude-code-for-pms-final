# 02 · Super Hearing — prompts

**Context:** You joined Rook two weeks ago as PM on Dispatch.
Release 4.2 shipped on 12 August, just before you arrived, and
landed badly. Last session you built a context file and met the
company.

You still do not know what actually went wrong.

At the end of the session, type wrap up, and Claude Code saves the
prompts you wrote yourself below — not the starter prompt — updates
CLAUDE.md, and saves your work to GitHub. By Module 6 this file is a
prompt library built from your own questions.

---

### 1.

create a table of every pain point discussed. include severity and why it matters

### 2.

from a birds eye view, which complaints sound like annoyances and which sound actually critical? Briefly classify every complaint and explain the evidence

### 3.

give me this info in a simple venn diagram

### 4.

what underlying problem explains the largest number of complaints? Back your decision with evidence

### 5.

based on the tickets, how would you summarize the root issue in a succinct quote

### 6.

run the week-by-week test in the pings data: misses vs pings received for the responders who went quiet and the ones who gained.

### 7.

based off this new info, how likely is it that this is an issue that stemmed from what you were originally thinking and why?

### 8.

Compare the complaints raised in interviews against support tickets

### 9.

give me anything you've missed - think about it from the lens of what showed up after August 12 that wasn't there before

### 10.

so are support tickets describing root causes? Map ticket categories to likely underlying causes

### 11.

give me a quadrant with the numbers of biggest issues from interviews vs tickets

### 12.

I want a cause and effect map connecting reocurring ticket categories and interview complaints, and show me metrics backing up which underlying issues appear to generate multiple downstream symptoms

### 13.

go into more depth on the issues that neither interviews nor tickets covered much

### 14.

pretend the interviews and tickets are both not the full picture. using both sources, give a biref breakdown of the story that best fits the combined evidence using solid numbers
